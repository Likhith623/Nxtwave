## SLIDE 1: The Student and the Hook

### Who Exactly Are We Targeting?
- **Audience:** Final-year engineering students (CSE, IT, AI/DS, ECE) across Tier-2 and Tier-3 engineering colleges in India preparing for their capstone/final-year project submission.
- **Skill Profile:** Basic Python or web development knowledge; eager to build with AI/ML, but lack production deployment and architectural evaluation experience.
- **Constraints:** Limited hardware (standard laptops, no dedicated NVIDIA GPU, no budget for paid OpenAI API keys or cloud hosting), tight 4–8 week college deadlines, and fear of viva interrogation.

### Why Do They Care? (The Core Friction)
1. **The Choice Paralysis Trap:** Students brainstorm dozens of generic ideas (e.g., "AI Health Diagnosis", "Stock Market Predictor") but cannot determine which idea is realistic for their skill level and timeline.
2. **The "Month 3" Disaster:** 70%+ of final-year AI projects run into fatal roadblocks weeks before submission—missing datasets, unmanageable GPU requirements, or hallucinations during live professor demonstrations.
3. **No Peer Validation:** Students work in silos and have no structured way to stress-test their architecture before faculty reviews.

### The Campaign Hook
> **“Stop guessing your final-year AI project. Take the 60-Second Project Fit Test, stress-test your biggest failure risk before writing code, and build your working prototype in 60 minutes.”**

## SLIDE 2: The Engineering-as-Marketing Strategy

### Why Standard Landing Pages Convert Poorly
Most workshop campaigns direct students to a generic registration page asking for their email upfront. Students are skeptical: *"Is this just another generic theory webinar?"*

### Upfront Value Before the Pitch (Engineering-as-Marketing)
Instead of asking for a signup immediately, **Project Compass gives students an interactive engineering diagnostic tool for free**:
1. **Multi-Criteria Project Feasibility Analysis:** Evaluates 1–3 candidate ideas across 6 weighted dimensions (Skill Fit, Time Feasibility, Resource Availability, Scope, Utility, Testability).
2. **The Build Contract:** Produces a concrete engineering specification containing the project's single load-bearing technical assumption, concrete failure scenarios, MVP boundary, and a 60-minute build roadmap.
3. **Break My Build (Growth Loop):** Generates a unique, shareable peer-review link. Classmates can stress-test the project with edge cases, which automatically updates the contract and exposes the peer to Project Compass.
4. **The Natural Workshop Conversion:** Once students see their failure risks and MVP boundary, the CTA presents the exact solution:
   > *“Your plan is ready. Learn how to build this working prototype on your laptop in 60 minutes at NxtWave's free live workshop.”*
