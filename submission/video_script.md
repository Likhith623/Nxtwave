# 3-Minute Loom Video Script: Project Compass
## NxtWave Growth Intern Challenge — Round 1 Submission
**Target Duration:** 3:00 Minutes | **Spoken Word Count:** ~380 Words | **Tone:** Confident, analytical, growth-focused

---

### [0:00 – 0:25] THE HOOK & THE CORE PROBLEM
*(Camera on face — energetic, crisp delivery)*

> "Hi NxtWave team! Over 70% of final-year engineering students face the exact same nightmare: they pick an ambitious AI project, spend 3 months writing code, and then discover fatal data bottlenecks, GPU crashes, or model hallucinations right before their viva review.
>
> To hit our goal of 500 registrations for NxtWave's free workshop — *'Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes'* — I didn't just build a static landing page. I built an Engineering-as-Marketing asset: **Project Compass — Choose. Test. Prove.**
>
> Let me show you how it works."

---

### [0:25 – 1:05] SCREEN-SHARE: PROJECT SELECTION & SCORING
*(Screen share: Navigating to `localhost:8000/compare`)*

> "Instead of asking students for their email upfront, Project Compass gives them immediate diagnostic value.
>
> A student inputs their real-world constraints: their coding experience, timeline, team size, and available hardware — like a standard laptop with zero GPU.
>
> They enter candidate project ideas, or load from our sample library — like an **AI College Policy Chatbot** versus an **Overambitious 4K Drone Transformer**.
>
> With one click, our deterministic scoring engine evaluates each idea across six weighted criteria: skill fit, time feasibility, hardware resources, and objective testability.
>
> It doesn't just rank them; it explains *why* the chatbot is a high-feasibility build, while the drone idea carries severe timeline failure risks."

---

### [1:05 – 1:55] THE BUILD CONTRACT & BREAK MY BUILD LOOP
*(Screen share: Opening the generated Build Contract at `/contract/bc_demo_rag`)*

> "Once the student selects their project, Project Compass generates an official **Build Contract**.
>
> Notice what makes this unique: it identifies the single **load-bearing technical assumption** that could collapse the project — for example, *'Students will only ask questions answerable from official handbook PDFs'*.
>
> It provides concrete **failure test scenarios**, defines the strict MVP boundary, and creates a **four-stage, 60-minute build roadmap**.
>
> Now comes our viral growth loop: **Break My Build**.
>
> The student copies this unique shareable link and sends it to their classmates on WhatsApp: *'Can you find a flaw in my design before my viva guide does?'*
>
> Classmates open the page without logging in and submit edge cases — like *'What if a student asks about cancelled circulars?'*
>
> The system stores the challenge, updates the project plan with an architectural safeguard, and prompts the peer to test their own project or join the workshop!"

---

### [1:55 – 2:35] WORKSHOP CONVERSION & GROWTH ANALYTICS
*(Screen share: Clicking the Workshop CTA, showing `/register` and `/admin` dashboard)*

> "Now, the workshop CTA isn't an intrusive ad — it's the natural solution.
>
> The student sees: *'Your plan is ready. Learn how to build this prototype in 60 minutes on your laptop.'*
>
> Clicking through takes them to our workshop registration flow, complete with UTM attribution tracking across campus club channels.
>
> Over on our **Growth Analytics Dashboard**, we track the entire funnel in real time: comparisons completed, contracts created, peer challenge virality, and conversion rates toward our 500-registration target."
